## 📱 How to Install on iPhone (Safari)

1. Open **[https://mukpostproduction-debug.github.io/friday-puck-parlay/](https://mukpostproduction-debug.github.io/friday-puck-parlay/)** in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom (square with upward arrow `[↑]`).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (`[+]`).
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. The official **Bet the Show** icon will appear on your home screen and open in standalone full-screen mode!
